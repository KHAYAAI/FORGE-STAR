# Per-pass capacity (illustrative)

One NB-IoT carrier has 48 uplink tones. A 50-byte message with 150 bytes of NB-IoT signalling takes about 3.2 s of one tone at 500 bit/s. With 40% of tone-time carrying messages, one satellite takes about **6.0 messages a second** per carrier.

The busiest region is southern Africa: 60% of all devices are assumed to sit inside one footprint. Satellites in view are counted over Johannesburg at a 16° mask. Generated by `sim/capacity_study.py`.

## Satellites over the busy region

| Fleet | Average satellites in view | Messages a day, 1 carrier |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 2: 12 sats at 45° | 0.15 | 77,040 |
| Stage 3: 42 at 45° + 6 at 10° | 0.53 | 273,240 |
| Stage 4: 120 at 45° + 30 at 10° | 1.50 | 779,580 |

## Messages per device per day the busy region can send

| Payload and protocol | Fleet | Year 3 (40k devices) | Year 4 (110k) | Year 6 (330k) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NB-IoT, 1 carrier (8 W isoflux payload) | Stage 3 | 11.4 | 4.1 | 1.4 |
| NB-IoT, 1 carrier (8 W isoflux payload) | Stage 4 | 32.5 | 11.8 | 3.9 |
| NB-IoT, 1 carrier, deep coverage mode | Stage 3 | 2.8 | 1.0 | 0.3 |
| NB-IoT, 1 carrier, deep coverage mode | Stage 4 | 8.1 | 3.0 | 1.0 |
| NB-IoT, 4 carriers (phased array, Stage 4 payload) | Stage 3 | 45.5 | 16.6 | 5.5 |
| NB-IoT, 4 carriers (phased array, Stage 4 payload) | Stage 4 | 129.9 | 47.2 | 15.7 |
| Compact own protocol, 1 carrier (20-byte overhead) | Stage 3 | 32.5 | 11.8 | 3.9 |
| Compact own protocol, 1 carrier (20-byte overhead) | Stage 4 | 92.8 | 33.7 | 11.2 |

## What this means

- **Stage 3 with one carrier supports about 1.4 messages per device per day at 330,000 devices.** Hourly reporting for every device does not fit. A few readings a day does.
- **Capacity, not coverage, is the tighter limit at scale.** Passes come every few tens of minutes; the queue on each pass is the constraint.
- **Deep coverage mode costs capacity.** It lowers the elevation mask but divides throughput by about four. Use it for hard-to-reach devices only.
- **The levers:** a compact store-and-forward protocol (less signalling per message), more carriers (needs more downlink power or a multi-beam array), more satellites over the busy region, and pricing tiers that sell hourly reporting as a premium.
- **The financial model should cap messages per device** by stage. A device plan of 2 to 4 messages a day fits Stage 3; hourly plans need Stage 4 or the compact protocol.

*First-order. Rates, signalling overhead and access efficiency must come from the lab test-bed.*
